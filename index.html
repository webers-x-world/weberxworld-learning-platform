Important Rules

Weber's X World maintains strict academic standards. Please read carefully.

Live Lectures Only

All classes are conducted LIVE. We do not offer recorded lectures under any circumstances.

All sessions happen exclusively on Google Meet. No other platform is used.

Homework Mandatory

Every student must complete and submit assigned homework on time.

Serious Students Only

This platform is for dedicated learners. Time-pass students will be removed immediately.

Performance Standards

Students with consistently poor performance may be removed from the course.

Active Participation

Participation in each class session is mandatory. Silent attendance is not enough.

Online Only

Weber's X World is a fully online organization. No offline classes are available.

No Recordings

About Us

What is Weber's X World?

Weber's X World is a premium online educational organization founded by Salik and Munazza — two passionate educators committed to delivering real, practical knowledge through live interactive sessions.

We believe education should be accessible, high-quality, and results-driven. That's why we offer completely free basic courses — no hidden fees, no recorded lectures, just live Google Meet sessions with real teachers.

Our Learning Foundation

Kashiful Uloom Computer Class

Both of our teachers — Salik and Munazza — learned their core skills, courses, and teaching methodologies from Kashiful Uloom Computer Class, a renowned institute known for its excellence in computer education, skill training, and practical learning programs.

Kashiful Uloom offers professional computer courses, certificate programs, graphic design, web design, and comprehensive IT skill training — empowering students with real-world knowledge since years.
